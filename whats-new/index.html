What's New

What's New in IronBuddy Nutrition Tracker

What changed in each version of IronBuddy: Nutrition Tracker, newest first. The app is in closed testing on Google Play and is not public yet.

Last updated 23 October 2026

Version 0.1.8 (23 October 2026)

  • Coffees by name: Shot names a freddo espresso, freddo cappuccino, frappé, Greek coffee, espresso, cappuccino or latte as that drink, and the drink keeps its name in the list and in the logged meal.
  • Drinks start at a real amount: a protein shake is one scoop, coffee, tea and juice a cup or glass, beer and cola a bottle, wine a glass. Grams typed from a scale are still Exact; a size guess is an Estimate.

Version 0.1.7 (22 October 2026)

  • Build a meal: in My meals, pick foods from the list, set the grams of each and see the whole meal's calories and macros added up. Save it, log it again in two taps, and change its foods or amounts later. Exact when every food is weighed, otherwise an Estimate with a range.
  • Batch cooking: the first question is One meal or A batch (a pot eaten over several days). Say how many portions the pot makes and one portion logs as an Estimate; weigh the cooked pot, then your plate, for Exact numbers.
  • A clearer saved-meal sheet: the numbers first, then how much and which meal. A batch picks Portions or Weigh my plate.

Version 0.1.6 (22 October 2026)

  • Shot asks one thing at a time: what it is, the meal, the foods, the amounts, then check and log.
  • Labels: every number is named, and one the reader isn't sure of is left empty instead of guessed.
  • Say the numbers: tell the app the calories and macros, recognised on your phone (English).
  • Plate photos with your meals: your plate photos now show with your meals on Today and in My meals. You can rename a meal in My meals.

A small copy of a plate photo is kept with its meal on your phone only: it is never uploaded and not in any backup. Barcode and label photos are still discarded after reading. See the privacy policy for the details.
